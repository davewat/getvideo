Three stages, one paste

Every video runs the same timeline. You watch it move; you don't have to steer it.

Download

yt-dlp fetches the best video and audio up to the quality you set, then joins them into one file.

Quality cap · subtitles · audio only · SponsorBlock

Convert

HandBrake re-encodes it with a preset, so it plays on the device you have in mind and takes less space.

Every HandBrake preset · H.264, H.265, AV1 · or skip it

Save

The finished file moves to your folder, named after the video. Nothing is left behind in temporary storage.

Any folder · your file name · Show in Finder

Easy by default

Open the app, paste a link, press Get video. Sensible settings are already chosen: up to 1080p, HandBrake's Fast 1080p30 preset, an mp4 in your Downloads folder.

Paste several links and they queue up, one at a time, with live progress for each.

Advanced when you want it

Switch to Advanced for the full set of yt-dlp and HandBrake options, plus a box for any extra arguments.

Press Save as default and Easy mode uses your settings from then on.

Why I made this

I made GetVideo for the teachers in my life.

They find short, educational videos online that are exactly right for a lesson. Then the classroom has no internet that day, or the video opens with a run of commercials in front of thirty children.

GetVideo lets them save the video at home the night before and play it from their own computer: no connection needed, no adverts, no surprises.

Dave

On the shoulders of giants

GetVideo is a thin layer. The hard work is done by open-source projects that volunteers have built and maintained for years. If GetVideo is useful to you, the thanks belong to them.

yt-dlp

The community-maintained downloader, forked from youtube-dl, that does every download in GetVideo. Its contributors keep it working as video sites change, week after week.

github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp

HandBrake

The open-source video transcoder that does every conversion in GetVideo. Its presets are the reason a file plays properly on a classroom computer without any tuning.

handbrake.fr

Both rely in turn on FFmpeg, which GetVideo also uses to join video and audio. GetVideo is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

It sets up its own tools

On first start GetVideo downloads the three programs it relies on, and keeps them current each time it opens. They live in the app's own folder, not system-wide.

Install

GetVideo is not signed with Apple yet, so the first time you open it macOS asks you to allow it. You only do this once.

  1. Download

    Click here to download GetVideo. Safari unzips it for you; in other browsers, double-click the zip in your Downloads folder.

  2. Copy it to your Applications folder

    Drag GetVideo from Downloads into Applications.

  3. Open it, and close the warning

    Double-click GetVideo. macOS says it could not verify the app. Click Done (on older versions of macOS the button is Cancel or OK). Do not click Move to Trash.

  4. Allow it

    Click the Apple menu, then System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security. Next to "GetVideo was blocked", click Open Anyway.

  5. Enter your password

    macOS asks for your Mac password (or Touch ID) to confirm.

  6. Open GetVideo again

    Double-click it in Applications once more, and it opens. From now on it opens like any other app. The first start takes about a minute while it installs its tools.

Prefer to run it in your browser? Download the Mac Go version and follow the instructions in the README.
